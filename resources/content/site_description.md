**New: GeneTrek 2**, with gene-set comparison, your own lists, an [API](/api-reference) and versioned gene lists. [See what's new](/changelog).

GeneTrek brings together curated gene lists for neurodevelopmental disorders (NDD), including our high-confidence NDD genes: the union of the autism, intellectual developmental disorder (IDD) and epilepsy gene lists of [Korkmaz et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2026.10.003). It adds gene constraint, expression, and the rare loss-of-function variants in autism of [Rolland et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-02408-2). Search genes or loci, compare your lists with known NDD genes, and download the whole table as TSV or Parquet.

**For research use only, not for diagnostic use.** For a question about an individual gene or variant, go back to the primary resources described in the [documentation](/documentation).

**To cite GeneTrek:** Korkmaz et al. *Biol Psychiatry* 2026 and Leblond et al. *Mol Cell Neurosci* 2021. Full references are on [How to cite](/cite).
